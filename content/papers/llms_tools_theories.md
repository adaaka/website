Sudeep Bhatia (University of Pennsylvania) and Ada Aka (Stanford University)

Large language models (LLMs) are creating new opportunities for judgment and decision making research. We organize these opportunities around two broad roles. First, LLMs can serve as tools to observe, code, and predict human behavior, making empirical research faster, easier, and more scalable. Second, LLMs can inform theories of the psychological processes underlying behavior, either by supplying representations of knowledge, beliefs, and associations to existing decision models or by functioning as process models themselves. We review current evidence, methodological best practices, and key limitations of these use cases. Together, these developments promise to expand the scale and scope of judgment and decision research while raising important questions about validity, generalization, and the nature of psychological explanation.
